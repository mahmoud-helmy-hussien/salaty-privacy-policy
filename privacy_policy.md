2. Collecting and Using Your Data
Types of Data Collected:
Location Data (Optional and User-Controlled):
Precise Location: If You grant location permission, the Application accesses Your device's precise geographic location (latitude and longitude) only when You are actively using the Qibla feature or when You explicitly trigger a location fetch. This data is used locally on Your device to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction. It is also sent ephemerally (processed in memory only for the request) to a third-party Service Provider (OpenWeatherMap API) solely for the purpose of retrieving the corresponding city name for display within the Application.
Coarse Location:
If You grant only coarse location permission, the Application uses this approximate location for the same purposes as Precise Location, understanding the results may be less accurate.
If You use the manual city search feature, the city name You provide is sent ephemerally to the OpenWeatherMap API to retrieve geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude). This city name implies coarse location.
Storage: The retrieved latitude, longitude, and city name (whether from automatic detection or manual search) are stored locally on Your device using application preferences. This allows the Application to function correctly without constantly requesting Your location, saving battery life and respecting Your privacy. This stored data is not transmitted off Your device by Us after the initial lookup.
